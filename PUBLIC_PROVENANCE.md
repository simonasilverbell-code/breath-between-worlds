## Why publish hashes

A cryptographic fingerprint identifies an exact file without publishing that file. If the private snapshot is later disclosed to a reviewer, the SHA-256 values can be recomputed and compared with this dated public record.

A hash does not prove every scientific claim about the project; it only helps establish integrity and continuity of the referenced snapshot.
