# Public method

This page describes the experiment at a level suitable for public review without exposing the private world database or operational credentials.

## World loop

Each model-hour follows the same high-level causal order:

`environment → local perception → relevant personal memory → resident decision → validated action → environmental consequence → journal + memory`

The observer interface reads a separate projection of the world. Observer browsing does not advance simulation time or become part of a resident’s decision context.

## Memory

Each resident keeps separate persistent layers for:

- episodic history;
- semantic observations and hypotheses;
- social encounters and exchanged information;
- personal projects;
- changing interests and qualitative temperament;
- evolving abstract visual state.

Information acquired from another resident retains provenance rather than silently becoming first-hand knowledge.

## Local knowledge

Residents are not given unrestricted global state. Perception is local, conversations require proximity, and personal/private state is not automatically shared with other residents.

## Actions and physical consequences

The environment validates actions. The baseline world includes simplified material properties, weather, soil moisture, plant growth and construction stability. Physical consequences are not resident reward scores.

## No single success function

The experiment deliberately avoids a scalar resident fitness score, productivity score, social rank or economic objective. Rest, solitude, refusal and unfinished projects are valid states rather than failures to optimize.

## Control-policy baseline

The preserved public-evidence snapshot was produced with a deterministic autonomous control policy. That policy is useful for testing persistence, causality, memory boundaries and action validation, but it is **not** evidence that a culture or subjective mind emerged.

A language-model decision path is architecturally separate. Future LLM-driven runs should be compared to this baseline rather than mixed with it.

## Reproducibility boundary

The public showcase will publish:

- architecture-level method;
- timestamped provenance hashes;
- selected event evidence;
- selected resident-created artifacts;
- clearly labelled interpretation.

The public showcase will not publish:

- the canonical private world database;
- complete private resident-memory contents;
- operational credentials or deployment secrets;
- private observer access values;
- claims of consciousness that are not scientifically supported.

## Research questions

The next experiments focus on whether preserved biography can influence future choice in inspectable ways, whether practical skills can be acquired rather than simulated by a fixed menu, and how richer 3D/spatial causality changes resident behavior without introducing a game-like reward ladder.
