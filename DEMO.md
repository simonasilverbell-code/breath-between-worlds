# Observer snapshot

The Observer is a read-only projection of the world state. Browsing it does not advance simulation time and is not fed back into resident decisions.

A short authentic screen capture from the preserved pre-LLM world shows the map, resident cards, event history, created objects, drawings, construction state and playable music phrases. The capture is being prepared for a later public media attachment without exposing private observer access values.

What matters experimentally is that the interface can be used to inspect a resident's history and the world record without becoming the director of that history.
