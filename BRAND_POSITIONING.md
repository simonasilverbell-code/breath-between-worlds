# Naming and positioning

The working public project title is **Breath Between Worlds**.

The internal world name / codename remains **LUMEN** while trademark review is open.

## Why not lead with `LUMEN AI`

The standalone `LUMEN` / `LUMEN AI` space is crowded in technology and AI. A recent U.S. TTAB cancellation proceeding involving the mark `LUMEN AI` reinforces that it is not a prudent public technology mark to adopt casually.

This repository therefore uses a distinctive showcase title and treats LUMEN as the world's internal identity rather than presenting `LUMEN AI` as a cleared commercial trademark.

## Current public line

**Breath Between Worlds — a persistent autonomous-agent world · codename LUMEN**

This is a working positioning choice, not a legal trademark clearance. A professional search should precede significant commercial investment, merchandise, filing or paid brand expansion.
