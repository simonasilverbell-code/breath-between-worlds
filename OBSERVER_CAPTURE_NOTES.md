# Observer capture provenance

The public Observer media is derived from an authentic screen recording of the running pre-LLM world captured on 12 Sep 2026.

For public release, browser chrome and access-bearing areas must be cropped or obscured. The visible content may include the world map, resident cards, event traces, drawings, music controls and construction status, but must not expose private observer credentials or deployment secrets.

This media is documentation of the interface, not evidence that any specific behavior is emergent. Behavioral claims remain grounded in the preserved database and event records.
